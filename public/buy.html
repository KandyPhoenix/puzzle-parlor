Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Tap "Save for offline" once at the top of the gallery and every picture is stored on your device, so puzzles open with no connection at all, on a plane or at the cabin. Entering a code and syncing progress between devices are the only things that need internet.

Which devices?

Any modern browser: iPad, iPhone, Android tablets and phones, Windows and Mac. Puzzles of 400 pieces and up are happiest on a tablet or computer screen.

Can I move the code to a new device?

Yes. Open the Unlock panel on the old device and choose Remove this device from the code, then enter the code on the new one. Three devices can be active at once.

I lost my code.

Search your email for "Puzzle Parlor unlock code". If it is gone, from the address you paid with and we will resend it.

Where are my photos stored?

On the device where you added them, in the browser's or app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded. That also means a photo puzzle is only on that device: to play the same photo on your laptop too, add it there as well. Removing the photo from the gallery deletes it and its progress.

Refunds?

All sales are final. The free version lets you try the app on your own device first, so please play a puzzle before you buy.
