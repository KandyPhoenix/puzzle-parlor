How it works

Pay securely with Square

Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your unlock code arrives by email within a few minutes.

Open the app

On iPad or iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, Chrome offers Install app. On a computer, just bookmark it.

Enter your code

Tap Unlock all puzzles at the top of the gallery, type the code, and every picture opens.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the app has loaded, puzzles play without a connection. Entering a code and syncing progress need a connection, but a puzzle you have opened keeps working.

Which devices?

Any modern browser: iPad, iPhone, Android tablets and phones, Windows and Mac. Puzzles of 400 pieces and up are happiest on a tablet or computer screen.

Can I move the code to a new device?

Yes. Open the Unlock panel on the old device and choose Remove this device from the code, then enter the code on the new one. Five devices can be active at once.

I lost my code.

Search your email for "Puzzle Parlor unlock code". If it is gone, email puzzles@phoenixmethodseo.com from the address you paid with.

Refunds?

All sales are final. The free puzzles let you try the app on your own device first, so please play one before you buy.
