Puzzle Parlor

Public-domain paintings cut into 500, 750 or 1,000 pieces. Your progress saves in this browser, so you can stop and pick up where you left off.

Sync progress across devices

Use the same sync code on your iPad, phone and computer and your saved puzzles follow you. Anyone with the code can see your progress, so treat it like a password.

The "New scene" pictures are original illustrations made for this app with Canva's AI image tool. Everything else is public-domain art from Wikimedia Commons; tap an artist line to see its Commons page. Piece counts are rounded to fit each picture's shape, so a "1,000" puzzle may have a few more or fewer.
