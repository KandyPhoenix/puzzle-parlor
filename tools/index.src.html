Puzzle Parlor

Jigsaw puzzles of spooky Halloween scenes, cozy Christmas and holiday pictures, famous paintings, fantasy worlds, fairy tales and landscapes. Pick any picture and choose from 100 to 1,000 pieces.

Pinned

    Sync progress across devices

    Use the same sync code on your iPad, phone and computer and your saved puzzles follow you. Anyone with the code can see your progress, so treat it like a password.

    The "New scene" pictures are original illustrations made for this app. Everything else is public-domain art from Wikimedia Commons; tap an artist line to see its Commons page. Piece counts are rounded to fit each picture's shape, so a "1,000" puzzle may have a few more or fewer.

    Unlock every puzzle

    The free version includes a selection of puzzles at every piece count. An unlock code opens every picture, and lets you turn your own photos into puzzles, on up to 3 of your devices.
